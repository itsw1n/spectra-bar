# SPECTRA geometry goal

[Reference image](../assets/spectra-bar.png)

Reproduce the reference's layered prism structure while keeping the compact bar, native Omarchy widgets, and popup behavior. Keep the Arch logo at the physical center. The badge uses a small hexagonal body, a darker inset face, a fine inner outline, and triangular shoulder facets. The panels beside it use matching diagonals and blue-gray edge accents.

The reference is 1920×51 physical pixels. The installed bar is 1920×40 physical pixels on the laptop at 1.25× scale (1536×32 logical pixels). The proportions are adapted to that compact height. Active content and glyph rendering vary with the running system.
